# Pre-pilot buyer checklist

For a useful first discussion, share only **non-sensitive** high-level details.

1. Business type / sector and country of operation.
2. CRM platform, number of monthly leads and rough sales-process stages.
3. Current pain: missed incoming leads, quotation follow-ups, callback delays or forgotten tasks.
4. Whether a **permitted test CSV export with anonymized data** can be prepared.
5. Who can manually validate flagged cases and provide customer-specific definitions.
6. Your target outcome: one-off independent diagnostic, process improvement, license or custom integration.
7. Any requirements concerning data handling, deployment restrictions or geographic location.

**Please do not send real customer lists, contact details, credentials, or confidential documents through public Issues.** We can agree a separate protected review procedure before receiving sensitive material.

No sale, partnership or expected ROI is assumed until interest and feasibility have been independently validated.
