# Commercial formats · Preliminary buyer discussion

**The prices below are unverified commercial hypotheses, not a legally binding offer or confirmed market-clearing prices.** Final scope, effort, delivery dates, tax handling and IP rights must be agreed after feasibility and cost assessment.

| Format | Provisional starting budget | Illustrative deliverable |
|---|---:|---|
| **Short diagnostic** | **from 19,000 RUB** | A narrow authorized, preferably anonymized CSV sample for one funnel; findings prioritized for manual review; limitations listed. |
| **Expanded lead-process audit** | **from 39,000 RUB** | Detailed review of agreed CRM export structure, rules, high-priority cases and reconciliation methodology. Scope quoted separately. |

| **Acquisition of digital asset** | **Individual due diligence** | Commercial terms and actual rights determined by documented chain of title and negotiation. |

**Not being advertised as available today:** a full CRM SaaS, unattended real-time monitoring, verified revenue recovery, or a turnkey installation into every CRM.

### Quality and acceptance

Before any payment or contract, agree:
- data protection and permitted export processing, anonymization and secure data transfer;
- the exact CRM schema, accepted signal definitions and excluded edge cases;
- demonstrable output quality against a client-approved sample;
- responsibilities, milestones, price, correction period and handover;
- permitted software use, IP terms and licensing.

No guarantee of additional revenue, closing probability, flawless outcomes or lifetime support.
